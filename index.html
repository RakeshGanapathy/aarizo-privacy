Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

Aarizo is an offline-first application. Your information stays on your device by default. We do not require an account or operate cloud servers for your personal data.

Privacy at a Glance

  • Your data is stored locally on your device by default.
  • No account is required to use Aarizo.
  • Aarizo does not operate cloud storage for your personal data.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • Optional features, such as the AI Assistant, only share information you explicitly choose to send to your selected AI provider.

Your Data

  • Stored only on your device
  • No Aarizo cloud storage
  • No account required
  • We never sell your personal data
Optional AI: when you use the AI Assistant, the content you submit — text, images, or documents — is sent directly to the AI provider you configure. Aarizo does not proxy, retain, or otherwise use that data.

Permissions

Camera
Capture receipts, documents and asset photos.
Photos & Files
Attach documents and images to assets.
Notifications
Deliver maintenance reminders.
App Lock
Optionally lock the app using your device's screen lock — fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password.

AI (Bring Your Own Key)

  • AI is completely optional.
  • You choose which AI provider to use.
  • Requests are sent directly to your selected provider.
  • Aarizo does not proxy or store AI requests.
  • API keys are encrypted and stored only on your device.
  • AI provider usage is subject to that provider's own pricing, privacy policy, and terms.

Backup & Encryption

  • Google Drive backup is optional and disabled by default.
  • Every backup is encrypted (AES-256) on your device before it is exported or uploaded.
  • Backups remain in your own Google Drive account.
  • Aarizo only accesses backups when you choose to back up or restore.

Your Control

  • Delete your data at any time from Settings.
  • Disable Google Drive backup at any time.
  • Remove your AI key at any time.
  • Turn AI features off at any time.

Your privacy is the feature, not the product.

Aarizo is designed so your information stays under your control — from everyday use to backups and optional AI features.
